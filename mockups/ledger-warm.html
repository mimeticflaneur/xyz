madrid/real estate finance × software

hey! i'm adrián.

i work in real estate finance and asset management out of madrid. right now i run the end-to-end financial oversight of a multi-asset portfolio — luxury residential, hospitality and golf — and i'm usually the person sitting across the table from the international PE firms that fund it.

before this i ran finance across itnig's holdco — venture fund, real estate fund and portfolio companies — did US-GAAP work at accenture in prague, and started out as an analyst at fundeen, spain's first crowdfunding fintech for solar. i studied real estate economics at the LSE, and i teach public economics and the housing market at ISSEP.

the other half of my time goes into building things. most of it starts the same way: some piece of analysis i had redone by hand one time too many. a few turn out to be useful to people who aren't me, so those i publish — a leveraged-return calculator for spanish real estate, a savings comparison tool, and right now an open atlas of spanish agricultural data. if any of that overlaps with what you're working on, write me — i answer.

Atlas Agropecuario Español in progress

Open data · Mapping

An open atlas of Spanish agricultural data. Crop area, yields, holdings and land prices sit scattered across ministry releases, regional registries and EU datasets, in formats nobody can join — so I'm pulling them into one clean, documented dataset and building a map on top of it, province by province. Both halves are meant to be public: the data to download and reuse, the atlas to browse.
