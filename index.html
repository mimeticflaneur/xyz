Atlas Agropecuario Español in progress

Primary sector · Mapping

A unified dataset of the Spanish primary sector, mapped province by province and — this is the point — comparable across autonomous communities, which the source data is not. It is built to carry real work on both the public and the private side: biosecurity, public risk and mortality measurement, traceability, and the identification of holdings, censuses and owners.
