consensus alpha, in plain words.

last updated october 2026

the idea

Consensus Alpha is a working paper on extracting alpha from decentralised perpetual futures. Its question is simple: where is market consensus structurally wrong, and how large a position should you take against it?

Hyperliquid makes this tractable because its activity is on-chain: every wallet's positions are public. The paper turns that raw activity into a sizing decision in three layers.

three layers

  • 1 · Wallet scoring. Not every wallet deserves the same weight. Each one is scored on its on-chain history, so the signal is a weighted vote rather than a headcount.
  • 2 · Consensus engine. The weighted positions are aggregated into a consensus, with exponential decay, so a position opened today counts for more than one opened weeks ago.
  • 3 · Fractional Kelly execution. The consensus becomes a position size through the Kelly criterion, scaled down to a fraction of full Kelly to trade some growth for much lower variance.
