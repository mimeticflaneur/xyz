the basics

A short manual for anyone about to work with me, or just curious: who I am, how I work, and what to expect.

  • Adrián. Based in Madrid. Spanish and English, either is fine.
  • Day job: real estate finance and asset management — luxury residential, hospitality and golf, alongside international private equity.
  • Also: I teach public economics and the housing market at ISSEP, and I build software for the parts of the job that should not be done by hand.
  • Path: business administration in Ávila, with stops in Köln and San Diego; consulting in Prague; finance at a HoldCo; then real estate. LSE and ISSEP along the way.

how i work

  • If I rebuild something twice, it becomes a tool. The RE Investment Analyzer is the Excel model I kept rebuilding, made permanent.
  • Numbers first, then the story. I trust a cashflow before a narrative, and I like being shown where a model breaks.
  • Language models for the rules, people for the judgement. I hand to LLMs what used to be a hand-maintained ruleset, and keep the calls that need context.
  • Publish what is useful. Most of what I build stays in-house; what could help someone else goes public.

reaching me

  • Email is the best channel: adrian@zapatera.xyz. Put the ask in the first line.
  • For the atlas or the paper, tell me what you would use it for — it is the fastest way to get access.
  • Happy to talk about real estate deals, financial models, housing policy or putting LLMs to work in a finance team.
